Account privacy and access notice

This notice applies to the invitation-only test release. Only approved testers can create accounts.

Your account and security
We use your email address, account status, selected review type, acceptance of this notice and sign-in/activity records to provide account access, security and support. Supabase handles passwords, email verification, authentication sessions and optional authenticator security. It also records technical security information such as IP address and browser/device details. Hostinger hosts this website and records website requests.

Authorized staff can view account details, sign-in counts and estimated activity/session duration. These estimates are not proof that someone is watching a page. Staff lookup requires separate authorization and two-factor sign-in. Successful staff lookups are logged. This tab uses browser session storage to keep you signed in. Authenticator-app two-factor security is optional. An emailed sign-in code is currently required for selected pilot accounts and cannot be turned off for them; other test accounts are not asked for one yet.

What this release does not do
This test release creates an email-verified account and lets you add optional profile details, goal answers and areas of interest (see the test Privacy Notice). Identity verification remains pending. Photo ID, financial documents, analysis and payments are unavailable, and creating an account does not grant those services. We will ask you to review the applicable notices before document services are enabled.

Keep credentials private and use your own email address. Do not email passwords, authenticator setup keys, photo ID or financial documents.

Account closure and records
There is no self-service account-deletion feature or automatic deletion schedule in this test release. For account support or to request account closure, contact support@financialtherapy360.com. Closure requests are reviewed manually to confirm they come from the account holder. This notice does not promise a fixed completion time. Account and security records may remain until that review and any required legal or security retention are addressed. Provider logs and residual backup copies follow separate retention rules, so closing an account does not mean immediate erasure from every provider copy.
