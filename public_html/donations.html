Community Support

Access should not depend on ability to pay

We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to improve their financial well-being. Community Support is the program we are building to extend access to qualifying individuals and families who may otherwise face financial barriers.

Our approach

What we are building toward

Open to more people

Help with understanding your finances should not be limited to those who can easily pay for it.

Clear and fair

Qualification rules and contribution terms will be published in plain language before the program opens.

Careful with your information

We will never ask for passwords, card numbers or bank numbers by message or through an unsecured form.

Where things stand

The program is not open yet - questions are welcome

We are not accepting donations or applications at this time, and this page does not collect payments. Qualification rules, contribution terms and payment arrangements will be posted here before the program opens. In the meantime, you can send us a question about the program below and we will reply by email.

Please do not include account passwords, card or bank numbers, financial documents or photo IDs. FinancialTherapy360 will never ask for these by message or through an unsecured form. Sending this form does not register you for the program or affect any future qualification.
