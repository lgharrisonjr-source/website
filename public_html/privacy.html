Test Privacy Notice (pilot)

What we collect

Your email address and password (the password is held by our sign-in provider, Supabase, and we cannot read it). Sign-in and security records, including IP address, browser/device details and session records. Only if you choose to add them: mailing address, phone number, how you heard about us, your goal answers and areas of interest. Records of your acceptances and your email-update choice, each with version and time. Please use sample, non-sensitive answers for your address and goals. We do not collect uploaded documents, photo ID, selfies or financial documents in this test.

Why

To run and secure your account, save your progress, support you, and send email updates only if you opted in.

Who can see it

Authorized FinancialTherapy360 staff can look up account details such as email, status and sign-in activity. Your profile and goal answers are not shown in that staff account lookup in this test. The providers and site operators that run the test can technically reach stored data to run the service: Supabase (sign-in and database) and Hostinger (website hosting and email sending from no-reply@financialtherapy360.com). Account lookup needs separate authorization and two-factor sign-in, and successful lookups are logged.

Your choices and keeping data

Change your answers and turn off email updates in your dashboard at any time. There is no self-service account deletion or automatic deletion schedule in this test. To ask for access, correction or closure, email support@financialtherapy360.com. Closure requests are reviewed manually and no completion time is promised. Provider logs and backup copies follow their own retention rules.

Security

Access controls apply, but no system is perfectly secure. Do not email passwords, photo ID or financial documents.
