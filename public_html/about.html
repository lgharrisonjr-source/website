About us

Our mission

At FinancialTherapy360, our mission is to help people make the most of their financial lives.

We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to understand their finances, make informed decisions and build a more secure future—regardless of their income, circumstances or where they are starting.

Through personalized financial insights, practical guidance and a genuine commitment to putting people first, we help individuals and families identify opportunities, overcome financial challenges, protect what matters and make their money work harder for them.

We also believe that access to financial guidance should not be limited by someone’s ability to pay. Through donations and community support, we strive to make our services accessible to individuals and families who qualify and need additional assistance.

Because financial well-being shouldn’t be a privilege. It should be an opportunity available to everyone.

Community-supported access is a goal. The donation program is not operating and contributions are not being accepted.

Our purpose

We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to improve their financial well-being. Donations and community support help us extend access to our services to qualifying individuals and families who may otherwise face financial barriers.

Where we are

A product in development, built carefully

FinancialTherapy360 is being built and tested before launch, starting with a limited first release planned in New Jersey. We are developing secure document handling, transparent analysis and human review first, because trust has to come before features. This informational website introduces the planned service. Customer services are not available yet.

Customer first

Recommendations serve the customer, with transparent assumptions and no hidden commissions influencing rankings.

Privacy by design

Planned controls include category-by-category sharing choices, revocation and deletion requests.

Explainable findings

Planned findings will identify supporting evidence, confidence and assumptions in plain language.
