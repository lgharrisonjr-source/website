Test Terms (pilot)

1. What this is

FinancialTherapy360 is testing its website with approved testers. Only approved test accounts can sign up. You are helping us test, and we may change or reset things as we go.

2. What you can do in the test

Create an account, add an optional mailing address, phone number and how you heard about us, answer optional goal questions, and mark areas you are interested in. Marking an area records interest only. Document upload, photo ID checks, document review and financial analysis are not available. Nothing in this test is financial, tax, legal or insurance advice.

3. Your account

Give accurate information and keep your password and any email codes private. Some test accounts are asked for an emailed code when signing in. Tell us if you think someone else used your account. Please use sample, non-sensitive answers for your address and goals. Do not enter anything you would not want a test system to hold.

4. Your choices

Everything beyond your email and password is optional. You can change answers, and turn off email updates from your dashboard at any time.

5. Changes and contact

When final terms are ready you will be asked to review and accept them before you save more. Each acceptance is recorded with its version, time and where it was given. Contact: support@financialtherapy360.com.
