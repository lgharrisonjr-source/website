Frequently asked questions

What is FinancialTherapy360?

FinancialTherapy360 is being developed to help people understand their finances, explore their options and plan their next steps.

Can I create an account or upload documents?

Not yet. Registration, uploads, analysis and payments are coming soon. This website provides information only. Please do not send financial records or photo IDs.

Is FinancialTherapy360 a bank or financial advisor?

No. It is not a bank, broker-dealer, insurance carrier, credit bureau, debt settlement company or automated money manager. The planned service focuses on financial organization and education, not investment, tax or legal advice.

What will it cost?

Pricing and paid-service terms are not final. The planned introductory offer is one free review from the six listed categories. It is not available to redeem yet. No payment is accepted on this site.

When will customer services open?

A launch date has not been announced. Customer services will open after the required systems, privacy notices and safeguards are in place.

Can I donate?

The community support program is a goal, not an active program. Donations are not being accepted.
