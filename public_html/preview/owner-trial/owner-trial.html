FT360 private owner trial

Private trial: no upload, no intentional saving. This page asks you to go fully offline BEFORE you pick any photo, and the whole check then runs with no connection at all. Your photos and results are not uploaded and are not intentionally stored anywhere. Press Reset to clear them, and close this tab before going back online.
What this trial can and cannot show. It shows whether the free matching tools work with your real license and your real face: barcode read, an issuer-code signal, front-text read, face comparison. It does NOT prove the card is genuine (a fake card can carry a valid barcode), does NOT prove a live person (no liveness check), and a browser match score is a signal, not identity verification. Reading a photographed license barcode and matching a small printed ID photo against a phone selfie are the two things this trial is genuinely testing - results here are real-world evidence, good or bad.
