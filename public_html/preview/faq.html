FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is FinancialTherapy360?

FinancialTherapy360 is a consumer platform that helps you assemble and understand your financial information, identify potential gaps and opportunities, and plan your next steps. It combines documents you choose to share, information you enter, transparent calculations and clear explanations to produce a prioritized action plan.

Is FinancialTherapy360 a bank or financial advisor?

No. It is not a bank, broker-dealer, insurance carrier, credit bureau, debt settlement company or automated money manager. It provides organization and education, not investment, tax or legal advice. Questions that call for a licensed professional are referred to one.

How does it work?

You create a secure account, choose which categories of information to share, upload documents or enter details, review what the platform extracts, and receive an explained, prioritized plan. See the full walkthrough.

What documents will I be able to use?

Paystubs, account and card statements, a credit report you request yourself, insurance policies and retirement statements. You pick the categories; you never have to share everything.

How will my information be protected?

The platform is being designed for encryption in transit and at rest, purpose-based consent, strict limits on internal access, and audit logging of privileged activity. Documents you upload stay private to you and anyone you explicitly authorize.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Deletion controls are a core feature, not an afterthought. Requesting deletion stops new processing and starts the disclosed retention and deletion workflow, and you can see its status.

Can my partner and I use it together?

Yes, through the optional household view. Each adult creates their own account and separately authorizes which information to share. Household membership never grants blanket access to anyone’s private records.

What will it cost?

Pricing is not final yet. We are testing a one-time financial review and an optional membership, and final pricing will be published before launch. We are also working toward donation-supported access for qualifying individuals and families, because financial guidance should not be limited by ability to pay.

When does it launch?

FinancialTherapy360 is in development, with a limited first release planned in New Jersey. This website is a design preview of the planned experience.

How do recommendations work?

Each recommendation includes an explanation, the evidence behind it, its assumptions, an estimated impact range where defensible, a confidence label and possible trade-offs. Recommendations are never ranked by undisclosed referral compensation, and uncertain or high-impact findings go to qualified human reviewers.
