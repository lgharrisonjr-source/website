Donations & community support

Access should not depend on ability to pay

We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to improve their financial well-being. The planned donation program aims to extend access to our services to qualifying individuals and families who may otherwise face financial barriers.

What donations will fund

Donation-supported memberships for people who qualify, so cost is not the reason someone goes without a clear view of their finances.

How qualification works

The proposed design would let members request donation-supported access from their account. The qualification rules and how funds are allocated will be published before the program opens.

Where the program stands

The donation program is being designed and is not operating yet. We are not accepting or processing donations at this time.

How to contribute

Register your interest

When the program opens, contributions will be accepted through a secure payment provider with clear terms about how funds are used. The inquiry form below shows the proposed way to ask about supporting the program. It is not connected and cannot register your interest yet.

1. Say you are interested

The donation contact form below is a preview of the planned inquiry flow. Tell us whether you are an individual, a family, or an organization.

2. Review the terms

Before any contribution is accepted, you will see how donations are used, how qualifying members are chosen, and how reporting works.

3. Contribute securely

Contributions will be processed by a secure payment provider. FinancialTherapy360 will never ask for card or bank details by email, text or an unsecured form.
