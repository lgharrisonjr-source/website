Brand

Five logo concepts

Original concepts in the site's navy and teal palette. Pick a direction and the final artwork gets produced, including a reversed version for the dark site header and small sizes for browser icons.

1. Full circle

A 360-degree ring with an arrow of progress, wrapped around a rising line: the whole financial picture, moving up.

2. Pulse

A heartbeat line that climbs into a trend: financial health, cared for over time.

3. Compass

A 360-degree dial with a needle pointing up and ahead: guidance in every direction of your financial life.

4. Monogram

A calm FT badge with a 360 accent: simple, confident, and easy to recognize at small sizes.

5. Growth

A sprout inside a circle: money tended with care, growing over time.
